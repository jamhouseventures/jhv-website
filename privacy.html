Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what information Jam House Ventures LLC handles and what we do with it.

This website

This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or third-party scripts or fonts, and it has no forms or accounts.

Like nearly all websites, it runs on hosting and network providers that automatically process technical information, such as IP addresses, to deliver pages and keep them secure.

If you email us

We receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it to reply to you.

Our email is provided by Google Workspace, which stores messages on our behalf. We keep emails for as long as we need them to help you and to keep reasonable business records, and then delete them.

Our recipe app

Our recipe app isn’t available yet, so it doesn’t collect any information. Before it launches, we’ll update this policy to explain what the app collects, why, who it’s shared with and how long it’s kept.

Your rights

You can email us at any time to ask what information we have about you, or to have it corrected or deleted. We’ll respond as soon as we reasonably can.

This website isn’t aimed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll post the new version on this page and update its date.
